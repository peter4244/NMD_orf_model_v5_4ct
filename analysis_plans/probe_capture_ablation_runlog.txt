=== what the capture head is reading ===
  ON AUC, NOT ON A DIFFERENCE OF MEANS. Zeroing channels compresses the
  p_capture distribution -- the median moves from 0.0329 to
  0.0135 across these conditions -- so a probability-point gap
  shrinks whether or not the ORDERING changed. AUC is invariant to any
  monotone rescaling and asks only whether reference starts still outrank
  their competitors. The difference-in-means column is kept alongside so
  the size of that artifact is visible.

  NECESSITY (zero_*) measures a group's UNIQUE contribution and is small
  for every group when the encoding is redundant. SUFFICIENCY (only_*) is
  the direction that can support a claim: it asks what one group alone
  can still rank on.
