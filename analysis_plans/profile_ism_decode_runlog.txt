=== the chunk-assembly loop, which is the shape that would explain flatness ===
build_ism_bank inner loop does, per position in the block:
    m = bp == p          # O(len(bp)) scan of the WHOLE block
    for b in range(4): bc[m], bw[m], m.sum()
so it is O(positions x pairs), not O(pairs). Doubling the chunk doubles the
useful work and QUADRUPLES this. That is exactly a flat throughput curve.
