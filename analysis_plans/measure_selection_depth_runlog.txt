  So 11.2% of transcripts have their reference CDS beyond slot 9,

  THIS IS THE CHECK THAT MATTERS ON THE TRAINED CHECKPOINT. If the fitted
  p_k still halve the mass every slot, the model cannot express anything
  about the main ORF for those transcripts, and the bank will report zero
  there for architectural reasons rather than biological ones. If instead
  the model learns small p_k at weak start codons, mass reaches deeper and
  the concern dissolves. It is one forward pass to settle and it is not
  settled by anything measured so far.
